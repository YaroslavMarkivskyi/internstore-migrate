# InternStore help & policies

Customer-facing answers the shopping assistant retrieves from (see
`ai_assistant/help.py`). Keep each `##` section self-contained — one
section is one retrievable chunk. Edit here, then re-run
`scripts/seed-help-docs.sh` to re-embed.

## Delivery and shipping

We deliver to addresses within our service area. Standard delivery is 1–3
business days; orders placed before 11:00 are dispatched the same day.
Chilled and frozen items ship in insulated packaging with gel packs and are
delivered on temperature-controlled routes. Delivery fees are shown at
checkout and depend on the basket total and delivery zone; orders above the
free-delivery threshold shown at checkout ship free. You receive a
dispatch notification with tracking when your order leaves the warehouse. If
nobody is available to receive a chilled order, the courier will not leave
it unattended — they will attempt redelivery the next working day.

## Returns and refunds

Non-perishable items in unopened, resalable condition can be returned within
14 days for a full refund. Perishable items (dairy, meat, produce, anything
chilled or frozen) cannot be returned for change-of-mind reasons for food
safety reasons. If a perishable item arrives damaged, spoiled, past its use-by
date, or was transported outside its safe temperature range, contact support
within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo — we will
refund or replace it. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and
usually appear within 5–10 business days. The shopping assistant cannot
start a return or issue a refund itself; it will direct you to support.

## Payment

We accept major credit and debit cards. Payment is taken when you place the
order. Card details are handled by our payment processor (Stripe) and are
never stored on our servers. If a payment fails, the order is not placed and
no funds are captured — you can retry with the same or a different card. For
cash-on-delivery orders, where offered, payment is collected by the courier
at handover and the order is marked paid afterwards. You will always receive
an emailed receipt once payment succeeds.

## Product safety and the cold chain

Every chilled and frozen product has a defined safe temperature range. Our
warehouses and delivery vehicles are temperature-monitored continuously. If
a product is exposed to a temperature outside its safe range at any point in
the chain, it is pulled from sale and any affected order is flagged for
refund or replacement — you do not need to notice this yourself. Product
pages show the storage temperature range for chilled and frozen items;
follow it once the delivery arrives. If you are unsure whether a delivered
item is still safe to eat, do not risk it — contact support.

## Accounts, orders, and the shopping assistant

Sign in to see your order history and track current orders. The shopping
assistant can search the catalogue, check stock, suggest alternatives, look
up your own past orders, and build your cart. It cannot check out, take
payment, or cancel or change an order that has already been placed — for any
of those, review your cart and check out yourself, or contact support for an
existing order. Your conversation with the assistant is saved so a human
support agent can pick it up if needed.
